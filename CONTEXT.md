## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This platform is a specialized, authentic Computer-Based Test (CBT) replica designed for **Bachelor of Veterinary Science & Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc. & A.H.)** students who are proactively preparing for the **ICAR AIEEA PG (All India Entrance Examination for Admission to Master’s Degree Programmes in Veterinary & Animal Sciences)** to secure **All India Rank 1 (AIR 1)**.

### Why Start in 2nd Year?
- **Avoid 5th Year Overwhelm**: Waiting until the final year forces students to cram 5 professional years of intense syllabus alongside compulsory rotational clinical internships, ambulatory duties, and nocturnal emergency shifts.
- **Consolidate 1st Year Fundamentals**: First-year subjects (*Veterinary Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, and Livestock Production Management - LPM*) form the foundation for all paraclinical and clinical concepts. Without consistent MCQ practice, these subjects are forgotten.
- **Active Mastery of 2nd Year Subjects**: Second-year subjects (*Veterinary Pathology, Microbiology, Parasitology, Animal Genetics & Breeding, and Animal Nutrition*) carry the highest weightage in diagnostic and production questions in the ICAR PG examination. Practicing high-yield MCQs concurrently with college coursework cements lifelong conceptual mastery.

## 3. The Official 5-State NTA Question Palette

In the actual NTA exam, every question on the right-hand palette is color-coded and shaped into one of 5 distinct states:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                           OFFICIAL NTA CBT LEGEND                           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  [ White / Grey Box ]          1. Not Visited                               │
│                                You have not visited the question yet.       │
│                                                                             │
│  [ Red / Orange Box ]          2. Not Answered                              │
│                                Visited the question but did not select.     │
│                                                                             │
│  [ Green Polygon Box ]         3. Answered                                  │
│                                Selected an option and clicked Save & Next.  │
│                                                                             │
│  [ Purple Circle ]             4. Marked for Review                         │
│                                Flagged for review WITHOUT answering.        │
│                                                                             │
│  [ Purple Circle + Green Dot ] 5. Answered & Marked for Review              │
│                                Selected answer & marked. (WILL BE EVALUATED)│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Bottom Navigation Controls:
1. **Save & Next**: Saves the chosen option and moves to the next question.
2. **Save & Mark for Review**: Saves the chosen option and flags it as Answered & Marked for Review (*evaluated for marks in NTA guidelines*).
3. **Clear Response**: Deselects the current radio choice and reverts state to Not Answered.
4. **Mark for Review & Next**: Flags the question for review without selecting an option.
5. **Previous & Next**: Move back and forth across questions.
6. **Submit Exam**: Opens the official NTA Examination Summary modal with section-wise question counts.

## 4. Subject Taxonomy (MSVE 2016 VCI Curriculum)

The platform focuses on 1st and 2nd professional year subjects, divided into the two official domains:

### Domain 1: Veterinary Science Subjects
#### 1st Professional Year:
- **Veterinary Anatomy (VAN)**: Osteology, Arthrology, Myology, Angiology, Splanchnology, Systemic Histology, Embryology, Avian Anatomy.
- **Veterinary Physiology (VPY)**: Biophysics, Hematology, Cardiovascular, Respiration, Digestive Physiology (Ruminant & Non-ruminant), Excretion & Body Fluids, Endocrinology, Reproduction & Lactation.
- **Veterinary Biochemistry (VBC)**: Biomolecules, Enzymology, Intermediary Metabolism (Carbohydrates, Lipids, Proteins), Bioenergetics, Molecular Biology, Clinical Diagnostic Biochemistry.

#### 2nd Professional Year:
- **Veterinary Pathology (VPP)**: General Pathology (Degenerations, Necrosis, Inflammation, Hemodynamics), Systemic Pathology, Pathology of Infectious Diseases, Veterinary Oncology, Necropsy technique, Clinical Pathology.
- **Veterinary Microbiology (VMC)**: General Bacteriology, Systematic Bacteriology, Veterinary Mycology, Veterinary Virology, Immunology & Serology, Diagnostic Serology & Molecular Diagnostics.
- **Veterinary Parasitology (VPA)**: Helminthology (Trematodes, Cestodes, Nematodes), Veterinary Entomology (Flies, Lice, Fleas), Veterinary Acarology (Ticks, Mites), Veterinary Protozoology (Trypanosoma, Babesia, Theileria, Coccidia).

---

### Domain 2: Animal Science Subjects
#### 1st Professional Year:
- **Livestock Production Management (LPM)**: Farm animal housing and environmental control, Dairy cattle & buffalo management, Sheep & goat production, Swine, equine & camel management, Broiler & layer poultry production, Hatchery operations and biosecurity.

#### 2nd Professional Year:
- **Animal Genetics & Breeding (AGB)**: Principles of Genetics & Cytogenetics, Population Genetics (Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, gene frequency), Quantitative Genetics (heritability, repeatability, genetic correlations), Selection methods and breeding values (BLUP, Selection Index), Inbreeding and crossbreeding systems, Conservation of indigenous livestock breeds.
- **Animal Nutrition (ANN)**: Principles of animal nutrition and nutrient digestion, Feed evaluation and energy partitioning (TDN, ME, NE), Feedstuffs and anti-nutritional factors, Forage conservation (Silage & Hay making), Applied ruminant feeding, Applied non-ruminant and poultry nutrition, Feed additives and metabolic deficiency disorders.

## 6. How to Run and Use the Site

### Prerequisites
- **Node.js** (v18 or higher; v24 is installed and verified)
- **npm** (v9 or higher; v11 is installed)
- Any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Firefox, Safari)

### Step-by-Step Execution Guide

#### Option A: Running in Development Mode (Recommended for Daily Practice)
1. Open PowerShell or Terminal in the project root:
   ```powershell
   cd "e:\ICAR PG RANK 1 APPLICATION"
   ```
2. Start the local Vite server:
   ```powershell
   npm run dev
   ```
3. Open your browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://localhost:5173/
   ```
4. The application is now live! All questions, test sessions, and analytics are saved locally in your browser's persistent storage.

#### Option B: Creating a Production Standalone Build
1. In the project root, run:
   ```powershell
   npm run build
   ```
2. To test the optimized production build locally:
   ```powershell
   npm run preview
   ```
3. The terminal will provide a local link (e.g. `http://localhost:4173`).

## 7. Strategic Practice Recommendations for AIR 1

1. **Daily 20-30 Question Drills**:
   - Use `Take CBT Test` -> `Custom Practice Mode`.
   - Select 20 questions, set time to 20 minutes, pick the subjects you studied that week.
2. **Weekly Sunday Mock Test**:
   - Take a full 120-question, 120-minute mock exam simulating the real exam day.
3. **Analyze the Negative Marking Penalty**:
   - After completing each test, go to `Review All Solutions`. Filter by `Incorrect / Negative`.
   - Review the textbook explanation for each wrong answer to prevent making the same mistake twice.
4. **Subject Diagnostic Matrix**:
   - Check the `Analytics & History` tab. If your accuracy in any subject (e.g., *Microbiology* or *Animal Nutrition*) is below 70%, dedicate that week's revision to those chapters.
